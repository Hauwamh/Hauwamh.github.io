NovaMed Solutions:
Sales performance & customer insights

Using SQL to query pharmaceutical sales records, then Power BI to present them, I analysed NovaMed's revenue, profit, products and customers to find where the money comes from and where the risk sits.

SQLPostgreSQLPower BICustomer segmentationConcentration analysis

$71.3mSales revenue analysed
82%Profit margin
45%Revenue from top 10 customers
7×Revenue from 'Seller' vs 'User' segment

Findings

  • Customer concentration risk. The top 10 customers generate 45% of revenue, so losing one or two would have an outsized impact. A key-account retention plan is the priority.
  • Segment focus. The 'Seller' segment generates 7× more revenue than 'Users', which points to where sales effort and pricing attention should go.
  • Product mix. A small set of drugs leads revenue, while the bottom five each earn under $0.7m, making them candidates for review.
