NextGen Corp:
Employee retention & performance

NextGen, a growing tech company, was losing people and could not say why. I combined six HR tables (employees, departments, performance, attendance, turnover and salaries) in Power BI to find where attrition comes from and what would fix it.

Turnover rate by department

Sales and Marketing lose staff at roughly double the rate of Engineering.

Why people leave

Share of leavers by reason. The top two are both about pay and prospects, which the company can address.

Does pay follow performance?

Correlation between salary and performance score. Where the link is weak (Sales), turnover is highest.

Findings & recommendations

  • Sales is the priority. It has the highest turnover (32%), the lowest attendance (79.6%), the lowest average score (3.48) and the weakest pay–performance link (0.28).
  • Pay is the lever. 60% of leavers cite a better opportunity or low salary. Structured pay bands tied to performance would address the main driver.
  • Early-warning signals. I proposed monitoring attendance below 75%, consecutive low-scoring months and no pay rise in 18+ months, with at-risk employees triggering a 1-to-1 retention conversation.
  • Knowledge risk. Long-tenured staff (8–10 years) are concentrated in Engineering, so succession planning matters there even though turnover is low.
