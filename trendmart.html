TrendMart Retail:
Where did the profit go?

Revenue held steady in 2024, but operating profit almost halved. Using 24 months of sales, cost and campaign data across five UK stores, I traced the margin collapse to its root cause: discounting doubled overnight.

Findings

  • Price, not volume, is the problem. Units sold rose 10% in 2024, but the average selling price fell 8% (£26.46 → £24.39) as the average discount doubled from 7.4% to 14.5%.
  • The break is a single event. My variance model flags January 2024 as the only month where 6 of 8 metrics moved more than 1.5 standard deviations at once. Total costs grew 14% that month while revenue fell 3%.
  • Costs grew on top of discounting. Cost of sales rose 18% and operating expenses 13% year on year (staff costs +16%), against revenue growth of just 1.3%.
  • All five stores are affected equally. Every store runs at a 40–41% gross margin and 18–19% operating margin over the two years, so the issue is company-wide pricing policy, not one store.

Recommendations

  • Cap blanket discounts and target them by category: Accessories and Kidswear can absorb promotions; Womenswear and Menswear cannot.
  • Review the January 2024 supplier and staffing changes behind the jump in cost of sales and staff costs.
  • Track discount %, gross margin % and ASP monthly on the Power BI dashboard, with alerts on the thresholds used in the variance model.
